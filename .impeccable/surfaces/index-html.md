# Surface: portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: recruiter or hiring manager, often non-technical, scanning between candidates. Action: open the resume or get in touch. Proof: four real projects, two peer-reviewed papers, toolkit. Constraints: static HTML/CSS, full EN/TH toggle, no Facebook, no invented employer or metrics. User steer: professional, not nerdy; no terminal or hacker costume.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Breadth shown, not claimed. One horizontal spectrum, Hardware → Web, places every real project and paper where it sits. Refuses the hero-plus-card-grid portfolio and every tech-costume variant.

OWN-WORLD: Cool near-white ground, deep navy-ink type. A four-stop hue path (copper for hardware, rose for security, violet for ML, blue for web) is the only colour, and it means position on the spectrum. Hairline rules, no cards, no shadows. Schibsted Grotesk for Latin with Anuphan for Thai, a large tight-set name.

STORY: Who Teerachot is → what range → evidence along the range → how to get in touch. Plain-language band names a non-technical reader understands.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name at display scale (top left), one positioning line, status, then Download resume (filled ink) beside Email/GitHub/LinkedIn. The full-width spectrum, with six labelled markers, anchors the bottom of the viewport.

FORM: Range spectrum, grounded candidate 1 of 3 in the safer register (user-chosen). seed:eba5ccac6d3b6d76. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a marker lights its row in the work list and the row lights its marker. Motion: the spectrum line draws left to right once on load, then markers settle; reduced-motion shows it static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
